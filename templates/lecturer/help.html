How Present works

  1. 1. Open a course.
  2. 2. Start attendance when students are in the room.
  3. 3. Show the QR code.
  4. 4. Students scan and check in from inside the radius.
  5. 5. You get a presence log of who scanned — not an official class register.

What Present does not do

  • It does not import or store an enrolment roster.
  • It does not mark official absences against a class list.
  • Scanning a QR is not enough on its own — location and session rules still apply when sessions record.
